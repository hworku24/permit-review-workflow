What this does not do

Adding a discipline does not touch cases already under review. Routing is decided when intake is accepted, and reopening a discipline on a case whose reviewers have already signed off would rewrite a decision that was made under the old rules.

The SLA allowance is not asked for here. sla_policy falls back to the row with no discipline set, so a new discipline inherits its permit type's review allowance until somebody decides it needs its own.
